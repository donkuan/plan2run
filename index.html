Race predictions use Daniels and Gilbert's VDOT equations; threshold pace is about 88% of VO2max and easy pace 60-70%. Treat them as starting points: let the HR cap rule a sub-threshold session, run at altitude by effort, and reset the VDOT after every race. Two rules from autumn 2026: no single week far above your normal volume, and no all-out race within 4 weeks of a marathon.
